01 Why Streamly

Built for the way you actually watch

Your TV, finally worth the wall space

Stream in 4K HDR with Dolby Atmos on smart TVs, consoles, streaming sticks and anything with HDMI. Pick up exactly where you left off, on any of them.

  • 4K HDR & Dolby Atmos
  • Resume across devices
  • No ads, ever

Download now, watch in the tunnel

Save any title to your phone or tablet with one tap. Streamly queues downloads over Wi-Fi and keeps your place offline, so the metro ride is covered.

  • One-tap smart downloads
  • Wi-Fi-only queueing
  • Offline progress sync

Every screen in the house, at once

One subscription covers up to four simultaneous streams. Phone on the train, tablet in the kitchen, laptop at the desk, TV in the evening — no juggling logins.

  • Up to 4 streams at once
  • Unlimited device switching
  • Shared watchlists

A corner of it just for the kids

Switch to a Kids profile and the whole library changes: age-rated rows, a simpler layout, and parental controls you set once with a PIN.

  • Age-appropriate rows only
  • PIN-locked parental controls
  • Five profiles per account

05 FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Streamly is a fictional streaming service invented for this project. The site is a front-end portfolio piece — hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no frameworks and no build step — used to practise responsive layout, design tokens and accessible components.

It doesn't — the prices in the plans section are invented to demonstrate a pricing table with a monthly/annual toggle. Nothing on this page accepts payment or takes any input.

Semantic HTML, a CSS custom-property design system with fluid clamp() typography and CSS Grid, and small ES modules for behaviour. The illustrations are inline SVG, the hero posters are original artwork generated by a script in the repo, and the classic-film stills are public domain — every asset is self-hosted and licensed for reuse.

In the fiction: two clicks from the account page, no fee, access runs to the end of the billing period. In reality there is nothing to cancel — closing the tab covers it.

That was the goal. Landmark regions, a skip link, keyboard-operable menu and accordion with correct aria-expanded state, visible focus rings, a light and a dark theme that both meet WCAG AA contrast, and every animation disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.

Yes — it's MIT licensed. Clone the repository, open index.html, and change the tokens at the top of style.css to re-skin the whole page.
